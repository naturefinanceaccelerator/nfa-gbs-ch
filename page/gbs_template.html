Swiss Biodiversity Score Tool

Upload parcels as KML. Get the biodiversity footprint of each polygon, with every number behind it. The measure is mean species abundance (MSA): 1 = intact nature, 0 = none left. MSA.ha = hectares of intact nature lost. Method: GLOBIO4 land-use module, consistent with the Global Biodiversity Score (GBS), on the Habitat Map of Switzerland. Your file stays in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

    Method in short

    1. Each polygon is projected to Swiss LV95. 2. The Habitat Map of Switzerland gives the habitat type (TypoCH) per 2 m pixel. 3. Each habitat type maps to one GLOBIO land-use class. 4. That class gives the MSA of birds & mammals and of plants. MSA = mean of both. 5. Footprint = area × (1 − MSA).

    Central follows the GLOBIO4 classes. Conservative treats managed habitats more strictly (managed forest 0.70, sealed 0.05, mown semi-natural grassland as extensive use). The truth for a site lies in this range. Field monitoring proves it.

    Land use only. Roads, fragmentation, nitrogen, climate are not included. Open water is outside the terrestrial MSA. Not the official GBS of CDC Biodiversité.
